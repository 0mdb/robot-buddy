# Alberta Grade 2 curriculum (what CBE teaches), for learning games

Gathered 2026-11-18 by a research agent from official LearnAlberta / alberta.ca sources (plus
secondary resources that quote the curriculum verbatim). CBE teaches the provincial K-6
curriculum. Purpose: pick what Alexa's spoken games can practise with the older child
(Grade 2) and the younger one (Kindergarten). Not a design; see `docs/learning-games.md`.

## Mathematics (Grade 2; minor revisions 2026)
- **Number to 1000:** count forward/back by 1s from any number; skip count by 2s and 10s from
  any number, by 20s, 25s, 50s from 0; value of a set of like coins/bills by skip counting;
  place value hundreds/tens/ones (2026: three-digit, thousands removed); zero as placeholder;
  number line; even/odd (sharing into two groups); estimate with benchmarks; compare and
  order; =, ≠, <, >; equality on a balance.
- **Addition and subtraction within 100:** recall facts with addends to 10 and related
  subtraction; strategies (making tens, doubles); add several numbers in any order; check
  with the inverse; missing numbers; word problems (counts, lengths).
- **Fractions:** a whole or a set split into ≤10 equal parts; compare unit fractions
  (denominator ≤10); rebuild the whole from a unit fraction.
- **Geometry:** sort by two attributes (sides, vertices, faces) and name the rule; faces of
  3-D shapes as 2-D shapes; draw from spoken instructions or memory; slides, turns, flips.
- **Measurement:** length in non-standard units (bigger unit → fewer units); centimetres;
  personal referents for 1 cm; estimate.
- **Patterns:** increasing/decreasing (hundreds chart, sounds, actions); repeating patterns
  with a core up to 4 changing in more than one attribute.
- **Time:** calendar dates; compare durations; days/weeks/months/years.
- **Data:** ask questions, tally, table; pictographs, dot plots, bar graphs.
- **Financial literacy:** spend/save/share; needs before wants; earning money; limits.

## English Language Arts and Literature
**Grade 2**
- **Phonological awareness:** segment words of 5+ sounds and with blends (bl, br, dr, fl,
  str); sounds in 3+ syllable words; blend up to 6 sounds; add, delete, substitute sounds
  anywhere (including inside a blend).
- **Phonics:** the 120 most frequent letter-sound correspondences, in order: single letters;
  qu, ng, sh, ch, th, ck; long o, a, i, e, u; er, ar, or, ur, ir, -le; y (story); soft c and
  g; i_e, a_e, o_e, u_e, e_e; -ed as /d/ and /t/; ea, ee, ie, ey, ou, ow, ai, ay, oo, oa, oe,
  au, aw, oi, oy, tch, igh, wh, ph, ew, ue; air, ore, ear, ire, ure, are, eer; wr, kn; ch as
  /k/ (school) and /sh/ (machine); -tion, -sion. Silent letters; letters with several sounds.
  Source: https://curriculum.learnalberta.ca/cdn/resources/boards/public/implsupport/120%20Most.Grd.2.pdf
- **Fluency:** 175 high-frequency words automatically; expression, phrasing, punctuation.
- **Spelling:** CVCe, VCC, VVC; drop e before -ing; double the consonant before -ing/-ed;
  short vs long vowels; silent consonants; 300 high-frequency words; a vowel in every syllable.
- **Grammar:** pronouns, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions; subject of a sentence;
  subject-verb agreement; exclamations; contractions; capitals for names, places, days,
  months, titles.
- **Vocabulary:** synonyms, antonyms, homographs; alliteration, onomatopoeia; base words and
  suffixes (-ing, -ed, -s, -er, -est); compound words.
- **Oral language and comprehension:** oral traditions, respectful listening; main idea and
  details, connections, questions, predictions; fiction, non-fiction, poetry.

**Kindergarten phonological awareness:** beginning/middle/end sounds; sort by first or last
sound; one-syllable rhymes; count words in a 3-4 word sentence; count syllables (1-3); split
and blend compound words, syllables, onset-rime, sounds; delete a word, syllable, onset or
rime.

**Grade 1:** alliteration (tongue twisters); rhymes up to 3 syllables; segment/blend up to 5
sounds; add, delete, substitute sounds in one-syllable words; first 60 letter-sound
correspondences (assumed to be the first 60 of the list above; unverified).

## Science (Grade 2)
Materials (natural vs processed; transparency, absorbency, flexibility, reflection; choosing
for a purpose); light and sound (vibration; light sources; light travels straight, reflects,
refracts); Earth (landforms; oceans, lakes, rivers, glaciers, wetlands; creeks → rivers →
ocean; fresh vs salt water; a year is one trip around the Sun; day and night from rotation);
living things (life cycles, offspring like parents, respect for plants and animals); computer
science (precise instructions: games, recipes, programs); asking questions and comparing data.

## Social Studies (new K-6, Grade 2 since Sept 2025)
Canada's physical regions and natural resources; prime minister and premiers; traditions and
heritage across Canada (First Nations, Métis, Inuit, francophone, diverse communities); trade
and transportation; democratic discussion and decision making. Detailed outcomes not found.

## Physical Education and Wellness (Grade 2)
Relationships; food guidelines and choices; safety decisions and consent (asking, giving,
refusing); safety at home, school, online, in emergencies; spend/save/share; self-regulation
of attention, emotions, behaviour; healthy growth.
