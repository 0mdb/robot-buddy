## Findings
- **Lengths:** three × 6 rows (12 screws) can be × 8: the insert pockets are 7.2 deep for a 5.7 insert, so 6.4 mm of engagement fits. M3 SHCS lengths drop from 6 to 5 (8, 10, 16, 28, 30). 07 amended.
- **F4b jack screw × 20 → × 30:** the gap from the jack block (x −38) to the carriage front runs 7–17 mm over the travel (taut: 9.3; belt creep moves it rearward), plus 10 mm through the block. × 20 only reaches at the front of the travel. 07 amended.
- **One driver:** every M3 SHCS is 2.5 hex. The canopy's ISO 7380 buttons take 2.0. Recommendation: **keep 2.0 there on purpose**. The canopy is the battery and electronics cover, and a different key is a small barrier for a 6-year-old (S3/S4). Bench-only: 2.0 (Pi), 1.5 (sprocket M2).
- **M2.5 for the speaker box (F12)** has no reason to differ: suggest M3 inserts (bulkhead) and M3 × 8; the speaker box isn't designed yet (P7), so nothing changes in CAD.
- Kit to buy: one M3 SHCS assortment (8/10/16/28/30), 4 × M3 × 8 buttons, nylocs, 49 inserts, 2 shoulder bolts, 4 × 624.
